## Published release expectations

One cohort of 20 participants completed four seven-day cycles; the program ended July 31, 2025. Daily video/journal targets were 560 each: 548 videos collected, 545 valid and 3 invalid; 550 journals collected/valid. There were 20 returned questionnaires and 20 interview transcripts. Journals had a 150-word minimum. Questionnaires were post-program, eight items grouped in pairs across PU/PEOU/attitude/intention; interviews had eight questions grouped 1–3/4–6/7/8. Worktile, WJX and Tencent Meeting were collection tools; iFlytek supplied transcription. Consent was oral and academic-only. No control arm or separate pre/post/follow-up dataset is documented. Table 1 compares V1/V4; exact V2/V3 prompts and item/rubric wording are absent. A request for two grammar/two phonology errors is not acoustic verification.

The article's publication license is CC-BY-NC-4.0 according to the publisher record. That notice does not establish a participant-data release. No linked record archive, native dictionary or participant reuse agreement was found in the paper/publisher record examined.
