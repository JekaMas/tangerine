## Frequency and relation semantics

Use the existing source/metric contracts and preserve all42grading equations. Additional source reports below are descriptive empirical statistics, not a new learner score. Per-leaf event rate is E/O only when native annotation covers all declared opportunities O and the population is explicit; O=0 gives N/A. Sentence prevalence is sentences_with_leaf / fully_annotated_sentences. Token/utterance rates must state their denominator unit and cannot be compared as the same quantity. Report native-label and mapped-leaf counts separately; one event with secondary categories does not become multiple independent errors.

For a declared fully annotated window population N and leaves A/B: support=nAB/N, P(B|A)=nAB/nA and lift=(nAB×N)/(nA×nB). Zero relevant denominator gives N/A. Example N=100,nA=20,nB=10,nAB=5 gives support0.05, conditional0.25 and lift2.5. Relationships are association, not causation or required mutation order. Store actual counts/window definition/source/crosswalk and unknowns. Unannotated populations do not supply absence labels. Generated uniform category selection is a generation policy, not an empirical frequency estimate.

Remaining account admission, local hardware/voice quality, real generated recordings, independently evaluated semantic judgments/Lab receipts, genuine participant populations and restricted source bytes are unobserved inputs. They are precisely scoped above. They do not require another product decision and cannot be closed by an approval or invented dataset. Missing or incompletely annotated empirical populations remain unknown/N/A in descriptive reports. Null is retained with its reason; zero is a measured count, not a substitute for missing data.
