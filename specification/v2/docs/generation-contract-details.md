the complete supplied replacement uniquely matches saved canonical wording,

## What is hashed and passed to the mutator

canonical_script_sha256 is SHA-256 of UTF-8 JCS of the entire saved case-candidate baseline. The canonical phase has no units and identical surface/canonical strings. Mutation preserves blueprint_id and the entire ordered utterance identity/speaker/canonical-text sequence; it can change surface strings and planned_units. It cannot silently reorder or remove a role/turn. A context mutation changes the relevant surface utterances, preserving the original canonical sequence; a wholly new dialogue is a new canonical version.

generation-input.mutation_recipes is the selected array of actual records from domain/mutation-recipes.v1.json, with recipe_registry_sha256 over the original registry bytes. Empty recipes are permitted for the canonical phase. In mutation, include selected renderable recipes for the requested families/exact targets and record the selection. Recipes are owned definitions/contextual guidance, while source illustrations/excerpts remain data. The recipe array/hash and effective context policy are part of the actual request and semantic-cache identity, not invisible side instructions.

## Exact mutation replay and event locations

mutation-manifest.schema.json is the private asset referenced by type=mutation evidence. It links the actual canonical and mutated candidate assets, their JCS hashes, recipe registry, actual selected recipes, seed/method, word counts, edit list and complete planned units. manifest_sha256 hashes every manifest field except itself; candidate hashes do not refer back to this manifest, avoiding a hash cycle. The server supplies asset identities and hashes, not the model.

Text edits use half-open Unicode codepoint offsets in the unchanged canonical utterance. original_text equals that canonical slice; replacement_text is literal surface text. Order edits by canonical start, then their explicit order for insertions at the same boundary. Apply against the original coordinates in one traversal, retaining unchanged slices. Nonzero edit ranges are disjoint; overlapping semantic obligations share one combined text edit with several unit_ids, rather than sequential offsets that drift. An insertion at the start/end of a replacement is ordered before/after that replacement respectively; insertion inside a replaced range belongs to its combined edit. Replay must produce the exact saved surface strings. Every changed slice belongs to an edit; every edit names its affected units. A whole-turn contextual replacement is permitted and retains all decisive turns/facts.

planned_units.text_spans use the final surface utterance, never canonical offsets. exact_text is its literal slice. Deleted-word targets may have an empty span at the resulting surface boundary, with their nonempty canonical deletion in the manifest. Acoustic-only targets keep canonical and surface text equal and have intent records linked to the unit and its final-surface scope; their exact realized typed operations are saved in the render plan. Controls have units but need no edit or acoustic intent. Several units sharing a change remain distinct only under the existing independent-repair-obligation rule. Copying a unit ID, alternative repair or secondary category never creates another event.

The server derives the ledger from the accepted candidate and localized unit scopes. If a text diff has several compatible alignments, retain the selected alignment/method and literal replay proof; do not call it native alignment. Unattributed changes or inconsistent scopes use the existing single output repair, then preserve the baseline and report the failed mutation. This is ordinary content integrity, with no separate reviewer action.
