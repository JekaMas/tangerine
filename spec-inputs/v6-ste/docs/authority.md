# Authority and scope

This package replaces the previous package. Keep the original v5 archive outside the active specification.
The original archive hash appears in `audit/baseline-lock.json`. Do not merge old defaults into this package.

The order of authority is:

1. The explicit instructions from the owner.
2. The OpenSpec requirements.
3. The closed contracts and domain registries.
4. The detailed procedures.
5. The reference algorithms.
6. The inherited test constructions and illustrations.

A lower source cannot weaken a higher requirement. If two sources conflict, stop and request a decision.
A test result cannot resolve a contract conflict. A research citation cannot approve a feature or a data use.

## Product boundary

The application prepares audio before a teacher starts an attempt. The teacher listens and writes one free-form answer.
The application saves that answer and evaluates it synchronously. The teacher can wait for the result.
The LLM gives evidence-bound feedback. The backend calculates named metrics from resolved decisions.

All eight language domains remain required. The interface does not require timestamps or a spoken teacher answer.
No live student, live mentor, adaptive course, or certification claim belongs to this release.
The application must not infer classroom management or teacher pronunciation from a written answer.

## Change boundary

The current request authorizes translation, review, and the explicit approval restriction.
It does not approve extra product features, artificial evidence, alternate providers, or fallback recordings.
Read `docs/change-control.md` before a proposed semantic change.
The change ledger distinguishes text changes, restriction enforcement, and unresolved decisions.

Existing model profiles and numerical policies retain their v5 values unless this review identifies a conflict.
Their presence is not evidence of empirical validity or separate owner approval.
A new policy value requires its own approval. A generated approval record has no authority.

## Knowledge boundary

The taxonomy defines a closed scope of 122 IDs. It does not claim to list every phenomenon in English.
A source illustration is not an approved corpus item. A provider document is not a successful provider test.
A schema cannot prove a semantic decision. The system must preserve uncertainty and the evidence needed for review.

Do not claim zero hallucinations, certified STE compliance, or production readiness from this package.
The release register states the remaining evidence requirements. A missing measurement remains missing.
