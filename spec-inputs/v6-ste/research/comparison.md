# Research comparison

This document distinguishes published descriptions from observed implementation. A product page does not prove its internal architecture.
The source register gives the original URLs. This review does not reuse vendor data or unpublished participant recordings.

## Moodrick

The public page describes spoken teacher practice, an AI student, and mentor feedback. It shows teaching-rubric indicators.
It does not provide a published confusion-matrix scoring contract or a licensed learner-error corpus in the inspected material.
These observations support a practice-loop comparison, not a claim about hidden source code. Source: `SRC-MOODRICK`.

The current product does not copy the live mentor. Its teacher submits observations after recorded audio.
The application must calculate its own stated metrics from case evidence. It must not present a vendor demonstration percentage as calibration evidence.

## NIR SIM and NIR OBSERVE

NIR SIM describes text interaction, learner profiles, lesson stages, and a transcript report. Source: `SRC-NIR-SIM`.
Text interaction does not expose the learner pronunciation that this product asks teachers to hear.
The inspected SIM page does not identify a model snapshot. Do not infer its provider from another NIR product.

NIR OBSERVE separately describes five transcript-based dimensions and names Groq for its processing. Source: `SRC-NIR-OBSERVE`.
That disclosure applies to OBSERVE, not automatically to SIM. This review makes no new live-browser performance claim.
Classroom behavior metrics remain unavailable in the current listening-only task.

## Doubao study

The paper reports action research with 20 participants over 28 days. It uses four seven-day cycles.
Its collected research records include 545 valid videos and 550 journals. These records are not project assets. Source: `SRC-DOUBAO`.

Table 1 requests at least two grammatical and two phonological errors. It also requests response latency greater than five seconds.
These instructions do not establish that each target sound actually occurred. The paper does not provide our required phone-level publication evidence.
Read printed page 123 for the prompt and pages 124 to 125 for the result tables.

The questionnaire reports behavioral intention at 50 percent. The interview analysis reports 80 percent.
These percentages come from different instruments. Do not merge them or report one as the other.
Neither measurement establishes teacher error-detection precision or recall.

## Task consequences

Keep explicit error budgets and observed evidence separate. Keep generated feedback separate from deterministic metric calculation.
Keep simulated-student behavior outside the requested first interface. Do not create teacher speech or classroom measurements without observations.

The full acoustic domain still requires actual audio and qualified review. No competitor page closes that evidence gap.
No study corpus becomes licensed training data because its size appears in a paper. Source use must obey the corpus registry.
