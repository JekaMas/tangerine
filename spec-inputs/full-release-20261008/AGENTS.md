# Work rules

Read `README.md`, `docs/authority.md`, and `audit/gap-status.json` first.
Read each referenced contract before you change its implementation.
The product includes all 122 taxonomy leaves in its first release.
The product includes all 27 main metrics and all 15 auxiliary measurements.
Do not reduce this scope to make a check pass.
Do not create mocks, stubs, canned provider replies, fake observations, fake credentials, or fake approvals.
Do not generate teacher gold answers and label them as human answers.
Synthetic learner speech is a requested product function. It is not permission to fabricate verification evidence.
Each exception needs a real owner message for that exact case, purpose, and environment.
No exception has approval in this package.
Do not add accounts, access roles, tenants, queues, live simulation, or a public API product.
Keep the local teacher workflow synchronous. Save the answer before a provider call.
Do not silently switch a provider, model, voice, dataset, source release, or measurement method.
Keep all required data sources in the release inventory, even when access fails.
Do not treat a key, account, or download permission as permission for every use.
Do not mark a gate passed without its actual evidence and artifact hashes.
Do not mark a missing source, unavailable interface, or unknown format as a completed integration.
Do not copy historical inspection receipts into the current result.
Before a destructive operation, show its exact scope and obtain confirmation.
Use short English sentences for prose. Preserve code, formulas, IDs, names, and linguistic examples.
Do not claim formal ASD-STE100 compliance from a mechanical check.
