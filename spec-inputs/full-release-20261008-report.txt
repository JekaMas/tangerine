## Your latest source decisions are incorporated

**EFCAMDAT R2, Shatz2020, and Öksüz2025 are separate required source profiles.** Neither derivative substitutes for the full release or the other derivative. Cambridge’s source page identifies these separately and requires approved access to its distribution. [EF Education First Research Lab](https://ef-lab.mmll.cam.ac.uk/EFCAMDAT.html)

**Moodrick and NIR have separate licensed-export or authorized private-interface contracts.** Configuration supports real credential references. It does not invent vendor endpoints or assume that an account grants access to private prompts, answer banks, or redistribution rights.

The full-source requirements remain visible when access fails. The application must report the missing source or permission, rather than silently substituting a sample and calling ingestion complete.

## Important defects corrected

The final feedback prompt now names the **model-output schema**, not the server-enriched feedback record. The model cannot create its own provider receipt.

Score transport now preserves the **exact composite fraction**, alongside exact component ratios. Display floats are explicitly non-authoritative and cannot become inputs to another calculation.

Linguistic stages receive the actual bounded taxonomy and context definitions—not just version identifiers that the model cannot resolve.

Acoustic evidence now distinguishes the **digest of a provider payload** from the **digest of the audio being assessed**. A phoneme or prosody observation must identify its exact unit; a shared utterance ID cannot transfer evidence from another word.

Retirement cannot clear an unresolved quarantine. Expired-lease recovery and cancellation have explicit transitions and stale-write protection.

The provider contract also preserves a material DeepSeek distinction: trusted instructions go in `instructions`; its documentation says that `developer` messages are treated as user input. [DeepSeek API Docs](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/guides/responses_api/)
