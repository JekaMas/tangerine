This is a pre-implementation trace of the active specification, not a new data acquisition or an importer result. All eleven original full-source scopes and all supplied native fields remain. Acquisition status below comes from the existing source registry/dated acquisition records; this audit does not re-download or re-hash their corpus bytes.

Supplemental JFLEG, ELRD, DiPlomat, EdAcc, DRAL, ICNALE and Speak & Improve scopes/locators remain as specified in `docs/sources/alternatives.md`, including missing linked media/access. Their data enrich selected contexts/references; none replaces an original full-source scope. Accents from recordings are reference evidence, not trained/cloned TTS voices. Current specified synthesis baselines have en-US/en-GB voices; further accent bindings require an explicit backend/voice contract, not a corpus download.

## Inputs that are unnecessary for the primary product

Academic identity, adopting source study questionnaires/scales, participant recruitment, full global mistake frequencies, official mirror-equivalence certification, every duplicate high-resolution representation, a completed full benchmark and human peer review remain outside ordinary setup/generation/answer checking. Keeping their original source data and optional reports does not make them launch prerequisites. Source terms and actual observations remain factual; removing a prerequisite does not fabricate data or rights.
