## Completeness manifest design

Each exact release has immutable upstream URL/release metadata, archive/file hashes, raw file count, per-partition record/speaker/learner/script/annotation/tier/label counts and original score scales. Preserve source annotator identities and raw offsets without double counting alternative annotations. Every normalized record cites raw asset, parser/mapping version and transformation. Keep unlabelled and excluded records visible with reasons; parser failure or missing file cannot disappear from denominators. Assess licensed storage, product use, redistribution, synthesis and cloud transfer individually.

Validate source population and partition totals against acquired release documentation; webpages cannot establish byte completeness. Small development samples may be explicitly identified during future implementation, but cannot satisfy complete-release gates. Do not combine an upstream test scorer or hidden test annotations into development prompts. Group related speaker/learner/script/case-family records before split assignment; original audio generation does not make a leaked source script independent holdout.

DOUBAO and vendor names have no acquired corpus contract. Their blocked status does not authorize invented records or removal from the requested inventory. An all-sources-acquired or complete empirical release claim remains false until exact authorized releases exist, or the user explicitly changes scope. Corpus acquisition is outside this spec-only request.
