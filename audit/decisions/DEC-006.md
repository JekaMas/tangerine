Status: proposed; not approved. Owner approval reference: null.

## Existing dependency

## Proposed behavior

Require a feature-to-method registry with exact feature ID, measurement unit, extraction version, interval boundary, review conditions and uncertainty. Unsupported features require observed qualified human evidence. Azure en-US advisory scores cannot supply arbitrary acoustic measurements.

## Exact boundaries and remaining source dependency

The recovered ground-truth schema supplies exact feature enums (lexical_stress, nuclear_stress, f0_range_semitones, f0_slope_semitones_per_second, syllable_duration_ratio, rhythm, boundary, weak_form, linking, assimilation, elision) and unit enums (label, semitones, semitones_per_second, ratio, milliseconds). Their presence does not supply the valid feature-method-unit pair registry; preserve these enums rather than replacing them. Each entry must distinguish continuous measurement from a judgment, require audio/revision hashes and retain the interval. Missing/invalid pairs, unvoiced intervals, unreliable estimates and unsupported varieties must abstain or require human review; never produce invented numeric measurements.
