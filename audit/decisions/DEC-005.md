## Proposed behavior

Version the spoken-token tokenizer and reviewed intervals together. Use reviewed surface word segmentation; preserve contraction lexical units; record special treatment of abbreviations, numeric expansions and hesitations in the reviewed transcript. Speech span runs from the first spoken token onset to the last token offset including internal pauses; articulation uses the union of reviewed active-speech intervals.

## Exact boundaries and remaining source dependency

Use integer microseconds for time boundaries and exact rational arithmetic for rates. Intervals must be ordered, nonnegative and within the audio duration, with overlaps merged only as specified. Proposed pauses are the complement of reviewed active intervals within the speech span. ASR/VAD alone cannot provide reviewed boundaries. Zero duration/token denominators remain N/A. Automated tokenizer/VAD settings stay blocked until a complete approved method artifact exists.
