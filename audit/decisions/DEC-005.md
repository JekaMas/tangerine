## Proposed behavior

Use reviewed surface word segmentation; preserve contraction lexical units; record special treatment of abbreviations, numeric expansions and hesitations in the reviewed transcript. Speech span runs from the first spoken token onset to the last token offset including internal pauses; articulation uses the union of reviewed active-speech intervals.
