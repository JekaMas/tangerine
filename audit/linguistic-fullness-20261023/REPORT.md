# Linguistic fullness and acceptable-variant audit

Status: completed for the finite LF-01 through LF-06 scope, with the acoustic and empirical limits below. Source baseline: `da6766d`.

The user requests another complete specification pass over mistakes and correct
pronunciation, including rhythm, blending, assimilation and different acceptable
English varieties, plus grammar, word use and collocations. Update missing
distinctions, test the finite defined population and attack the evidence.

## Scope and method

Inspect all 122 existing leaves, their recipes, exclusions, prompt/context
projection and scoring treatment. Preserve their identifiers, all original
formulas and source scopes. Research primary linguistic references. Distinguish
an error in context from an optional style, accent, dialect, register or careful /
connected-speech alternative. A pronunciation target is not a universal English
norm. No pre-generated corpus or human review prerequisite is added.

Maintain a per-leaf coverage matrix with literal error/control examples,
independent expectations, source references and explicit evidence strength.
Attack false-positive opportunities and weak proof (requested operation, schema
pass, successful transport, model opinion or copied key is not independently
observed linguistic correctness). Retain failed cases and narrow repairs.

Only existing local resources, verified free allowances and the existing
included-only ChatGPT connection may be used. No purchased credits, billing,
overage, paid fallback, new account, installation or external publication.

## Findings and repairs

Every one of the 122 leaves was inspected through its definition, original
inclusions/exclusions, context, illustration and recipe. The new active contrast
set supplies a canonical reference, contextual surface and concrete correct
control for each leaf. Its 518 subtype mappings refine the existing inventory;
they are not 518 separately qualified acoustic capabilities. No category ID,
formula or original source identity was removed. Original examples remain in
the immutable imported releases and Git history.

- **Implicit subtype/variant coverage:** make clause constructions, noun senses,
  collocation shapes, allophones and connected-speech processes explicit in the
  existing inclusion/exclusion projection. Correct variants reach generation
  and the mapper; the separate contrast file is development reference data.
- **Ambiguous or abstract illustrations:** 55 active illustration contexts/pairs
  are repaired. Examples include a/an by sound, a genuinely distinct reflexive
  error, a single pronoun-number obligation, a word-class example avoiding a
  permitted flat-adverb reading, concrete acoustic targets and actual pragmatic
  utterances. The complete before/after record is ILLUSTRATION-ERRATA.json.
  Three of these reconcile already-corrected recipe examples back into the
  active taxonomy: one consonant insertion and concrete reference/relevance
  utterances. Their model-visible contrasts did not change.
- **False-positive traps:** different rhoticity, flapping, glottalization,
  release, partial devoicing, vowel systems, prominence, rhythm, reduction,
  coalescence, careful unblended speech, intonation, grammar variety, word sense,
  register and collocation alternatives are explicit controls. Accent, a locale
  label or frequency alone does not decide correctness.
- **Missing explicit phonetic subtypes:** consonant metathesis/order maps to
  the existing consonant-substitution leaf as one lexical repair; vowel deletion
  and syllable loss map to elision/reduction. The existing phone-pair compiler
  already represents these contrasts. Scoped aliases keep the original primary
  ID. Attested ask/aks and two-syllable camera are correct controls in context.
- **Invented practice norms:** a requested local drill can have a defined target;
  broad pronunciation/CEFR selection cannot manufacture that target solely to
  turn a correct variant into an error. Artificial ms/density examples are
  labelled controlled tasks, not universal English standards.
- **Stale review language:** active taxonomy publication metadata no longer says
  gold or dual review is required. Alias proposal/approval-gap metadata no longer
  suggests a missing human approval. No new linguistic approval is fabricated.
- **Explanation versus correction:** accepted primary repair and detection remain
  separate from omitted/false reasoning. A plausible defective key can remain
  unresolved under the existing optional reconsideration route.

native context/control/arithmetic observations remain valid for unchanged test
