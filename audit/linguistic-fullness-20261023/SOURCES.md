# Research record — 23 October 2026

Primary sources were searched/read for the distinctions below. Cached search
extracts and directly available pages were used; several direct Cambridge and
university textbook opens returned HTTP403. Those failures were not treated as
full-page inspection. No corpus was downloaded or supplied to a provider.
Examples and expected labels in this audit are authored test references, not
quoted corpus records or new expert/human approvals.

Research did not establish frequencies for all leaves, all-variety exhaustive
coverage, a precise social meaning for every contour, or perceptual correctness
of our generated audio. Those are different claims from specifying/test-driving
the finite linguistic contracts. The ordinary on-demand flow needs no external
mistake corpus or pre-existing audio examples.
