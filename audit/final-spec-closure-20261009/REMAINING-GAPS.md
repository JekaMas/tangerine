# Remaining specification gaps

No product decision is needed for the four findings raised in the latest user message. They are resolved as written contracts:

| Finding | Current resolution | Why it matters to this product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EXP-002 key/rubric repair | Existing LLM adjudication receives the complete original/surface text, mutation manifest and answer; returns complete semantic units, repairs, rationale and correspondence. Native projection appends the key/result on unchanged audio. | The LLM already has sufficient information. The return/persistence shape lets the app actually store and reuse its judgment; it is not another data prerequisite or review ceremony. Explanation-only changes preserve supported primary scores. |
| EXP-003 recovery | Preparation/settings/phase artifacts are durable before calls and at completed checkpoints; explicit resume selects the exact saved association. | Restart can reuse completed work and its original settings without paying again or silently mixing models/prompts/voices. |
| EXP-004 contextual mistakes | Ordinary generation excludes instances whose decisive facts remain private or external. All categories use suitable self-contained audible instances. | A listener can diagnose the mistake using what the exercise actually presents. No separate briefing, private knowledge or category removal is required. |
| EXP-010 test-design completeness | Concrete constructed packets and independent expectations bind every current property/case; original formulas/category/source scopes remain complete. | Developers have literal inputs and expected output/state/arithmetic, rather than generic labels or self-defined LLM gold. New designs remain unexecuted. |

Future engineering observations are still necessary when implementation is authorized: actual semantic/provider results, generated audio realization, durable restart/readback/restore, native scoring/key repair and selected AI-development Lab receipts. These are execution evidence, not requests for another product approval. Static checks do not substitute for them.

Optional source/empirical dependencies keep their existing honest status under specification/v2/docs/product-data-relevance.md. Restricted/private bytes, genuine teacher populations and unknown per-leaf frequencies are not required for independent generated exercises. This increment obtains none of them and does not claim complete empirical coverage. All eleven original full-source scopes are preserved.

This is closure of the four identified specification findings and their supporting contracts, not a guarantee that future implementation or model experiments cannot reveal another defect.
