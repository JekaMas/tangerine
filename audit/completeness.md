## Material contradictions and underspecification

1. The inherited six-per-leaf/732 policy conflicts with leaves that represent permitted variants; multi-target assets and observation counts are not interchangeable (DEC-002).
2. Taxonomy illustrations are unreviewed references and cannot be seeded as exercises/gold. The current file set does not even supply those illustration bytes.
3. Global evidence ID membership cannot prove exercise/unit/revision/audio ownership. Independent authorization and database constraints are mandatory (DOM-02).
4. Structured JSON conformance does not prove linguistic truth. Audit/refusal, qualified humans and untouched held-out evidence remain required.
5. TTS request controls do not prove pronunciation/tempo/voice realization. Advisory ASR/PA outputs do not supply observed replacement phones.
6. Numerical thresholds/weights are policy candidates. Known formula strings do not reveal missing weighting/credit/threshold values.
7. The original approval-record schema is absent. Draft decision files do not claim conformance to that original schema or create owner consent.
8. Zero-denominator output reasons need the original per-metric counter semantics. The arithmetic helper requires an explicit reason rather than inventing one.
9. Timeout alignment needs a single propagated attempt deadline; three independent 270-second timers cannot alone prove the overall limit.
10. Backup integrity and actual restore are separate checks; both were required. Empty media/system metadata restoration does not validate exercise-audio corpus recovery.
11. Existing official MinIO community distribution is source-only and upstream is archived. The requested owner is retained by a source build, with its status documented; no substitute provider was introduced.
12. A task-derived graph with positive/negative definitions is an implementation plan. Planned/blocked tests are not performed tests, and no coverage percentage is inferred from their count.
