# Mandatory observable AI development

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: DEV-01 Mandatory Lab evidence for AI development

AI-affecting development SHALL use the selected local Lab and retain ai-development-run evidence before claiming observed AI completion; ordinary runtime SHALL remain usable without Lab. Specification-only and zero-call deterministic work SHALL keep their actual proof limits. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-01 Recorded AI increment

- **GIVEN** A mapping prompt/adapter increment with selected positive/negative cases
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** the increment executes through Lab-linked native owners and saves exact evidence

#### Scenario: DEV-01 Ordinary use or unavailable development evidence

- **GIVEN** Lab is off or the selected development observations are missing
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** ordinary learner grades remain usable and AI development is explicitly incomplete rather than reported verified

### Requirement: DEV-02 Complete inference surface inventory

Development SHALL reconcile every owned remote/local inference and wrapper/helper/plugin path against ai-surface-inventory, including all fourteen declared surfaces and future additions. Static inventory SHALL not masquerade as runtime coverage. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-02 Registered alternate inference route

- **GIVEN** An implemented speech/judge/alternate adapter path
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** the inventory identifies its owner/serializer/trusted fragments and separate actual calls

#### Scenario: DEV-02 Unregistered helper or dynamic call

- **GIVEN** A hidden helper SDK call or local model path absent from inventory
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** dispatch reconciliation exposes it and the selected development coverage remains incomplete

### Requirement: DEV-03 Versioned trusted instruction sources

Every trusted stage/schema/repair/fallback/judge/reference/adapter instruction fragment SHALL have an exact saved owner/hash. Dynamic answers/source/errors SHALL remain data, without hidden unversioned concatenation or recursive instruction expansion. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-03 Inspect all rendered trusted fragments

- **GIVEN** A request includes a schema description and semantic-repair wrapper
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** their actual text/role/order and source hashes are reconstructable

#### Scenario: DEV-03 Hidden instruction addition

- **GIVEN** An adapter appends an unregistered instruction or evaluates answer text as a template
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** the mismatch is observed and no complete controlled-context claim is issued

### Requirement: DEV-04 Exact model-visible snapshots

Each actual model call SHALL save the final noncredential request after all serialization/defaults and before dispatch, with actual part/asset hashes, modalities and framing. Non-LLM speech/ASR inputs SHALL be inspectable too. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-04 Inspect speech and LLM actual inputs

- **GIVEN** A JSON LLM call and SSML/audio reference call
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** actual model-visible bytes/parameters match their saved snapshots

#### Scenario: DEV-04 Post-snapshot SDK change

- **GIVEN** A serializer or SDK alters a default/role/prompt after snapshot creation
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** transport mismatch is retained as incomplete snapshot coverage instead of relying on template hashes

### Requirement: DEV-05 All attempt outcomes and caches accounted

Every initial/retry/fallback/repair/local/judge attempt SHALL retain distinct actual snapshots/receipts/spans, including failures/cancellation/late outputs. Cache views SHALL reference the original call without inventing a new dispatch. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-05 Repair lineage and cache inspection

- **GIVEN** An initial call is repaired and later inspected from cache
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** two real attempts and one cache reference retain distinct lineage and real usage

#### Scenario: DEV-05 Lost refused or interrupted attempt

- **GIVEN** An attempt refuses/errors/crashes after dispatch or an exporter drops it
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** the initiated attempt stays in the denominator and unresolved outcome/export stays explicit

### Requirement: DEV-06 Context disclosure and budget fidelity

Development SHALL exercise actual stage disclosure/budget selection: blind extraction omits keys/prior scores; independent audit omits mapper results; mapping/feedback retain required frozen inputs; ASR/reference conditioning remains explicit. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-06 Correct stage-specific context

- **GIVEN** A fixed answer/key and actual native result enter the selected pipeline
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** each stage sees exactly its allowed required components and explicit optional selections

#### Scenario: DEV-06 Leaked or truncated context

- **GIVEN** A custom policy adds the key to blind extraction, first-map decisions to audit or removes required answer bytes
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** the actual assembled context exposes the defect and no accurate-grade/independence claim is made

### Requirement: DEV-07 Variant binding and cache isolation

Prompt/context/fragment/schema/recipe/model/voice/input parameter changes SHALL affect the relevant frozen binding/cache identity. Editing/activation SHALL preserve earlier saved attempts and use the actual selected version, without hidden fallback. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-07 Changed variant executes its own binding

- **GIVEN** A selected trusted fragment or model-visible parameter differs from baseline
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** new calls use that exact variant and old answers/results retain their original binding

#### Scenario: DEV-07 Stale cache or alias substitution

- **GIVEN** A mutable alias changes or a cache entry has another effective input identity
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** the run does not reuse the mismatched decision or silently substitute a profile

### Requirement: DEV-08 Measured semantic change evidence

AI development SHALL select affected positive/negative cases and actual dependent pipelines with frozen independent expectations, preserving failed/unperformed rows, all122coverage reporting and existing exact arithmetic. Unsupported quality improvement claims SHALL remain unproven. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-08 Paired change with real failure counts

- **GIVEN** A baseline/candidate comparison on the same fixed reference population
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** paired metrics retain actual origins/denominators and relevant dependent cases

#### Scenario: DEV-08 Optimistic or circular evaluation

- **GIVEN** Candidate-made labels, leaked expected answers, excluded failures or unselected leaves
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** the report labels self-consistency/missing coverage and cannot claim independent improvement/full coverage

### Requirement: DEV-09 Observable and challenged judges

Judges/scorers SHALL use frozen rubric/prompt/profile/schema identities and actual trace/receipts, with correct/incorrect/unknown test packets. Judge agreement SHALL not replace native arithmetic or absent acoustic evidence. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-09 Judge verdicts have actual provenance

- **GIVEN** Known correct/incorrect/unsupported packets and a selected diagnostic judge
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** saved verdicts/receipts expose false support/rejection/unknown handling with actual basis

#### Scenario: DEV-09 Self-validating or invented judge evidence

- **GIVEN** The candidate grades its own labels or text-only judge claims observed phones
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** the circular/unsupported inference is retained and cannot establish independent/measured correctness

### Requirement: DEV-10 Private but inspectable diagnostic artifacts

Development SHALL explicitly select permitted body capture and preserve private original/sanitized hashes and redaction reasons. It SHALL never export credentials/private source fields or claim complete body inspection after an undisclosed omission. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-10 Sanitized context stays attributable

- **GIVEN** Permitted context plus controlled secret/private-field canaries
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** Lab copies omit those fields while original grading bytes and separate hashes stay correct

#### Scenario: DEV-10 Forbidden or missing body copy

- **GIVEN** Source terms prohibit copying a component or redaction/export loses required content
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** inspection limits remain explicit instead of fake full capture or an exported secret

### Requirement: DEV-11 Export outages do not falsify development evidence

Lab export failure SHALL preserve native results and mark AI development evidence incomplete; explicit sync SHALL export the same saved observations without inference. Restore/deletion SHALL preserve honest pending status. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-11 Export-only recovery

- **GIVEN** A completed operation has native evidence and a transient Lab outage
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** sync restores exact diagnostic linkage without a new model call

#### Scenario: DEV-11 Unsynchronized or failed cleanup claim

- **GIVEN** Lab artifacts remain unavailable or copied-body deletion fails
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** the run retains missing/pending items and cannot claim successful synchronization/deletion

### Requirement: DEV-12 Coverage completeness and precise invalidation

Development SHALL reconcile independently observed dispatches, native snapshots/receipts and actual MLflow exports using the exact selected property/case/dependency inventory. Unmatched paths and zero populations SHALL remain explicit; unchanged valid greens SHALL be retained. The owning definitions are specification/v2/docs/ai-development.md and domain/ai-development.v1.json.

#### Scenario: DEV-12 Complete finite selected evidence join

- **GIVEN** Observed transport/local dispatches and matching native/Lab records
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** the three-way join covers every required selected observation and names unselected paths

#### Scenario: DEV-12 False coverage or blanket rerun

- **GIVEN** A dispatch is missing, duplicate export replaces another, zero calls claim integration or an unrelated edit triggers all tests
- **WHEN** the selected development property is exercised
- **THEN** coverage remains incomplete/N/A as applicable and only truly changed dependency proofs rerun
