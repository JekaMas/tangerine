## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: I33 Saved-audio diagnostic and fixture repair
The application SHALL use the same owned model-visible diagnostic projection for local and Gemini auditory transports. Selected saved-audio research SHALL preserve original inputs, recordings, measurements and replies, permit explicitly ambiguous authored expectations, and separate acoustic properties from contextual correctness as specified in specification/v2/docs/saved-audio-diagnostics.md. This research SHALL add no learner gate, repeated stored-data audit or bulk judge campaign.

#### Scenario: I33 Equal restricted diagnostic context
- **WHEN** an ordinary advisory audio diagnostic uses either supported transport
- **THEN** it receives the selected intended targets, public context and actual target/reference audio through the same projection; private corrections, learner answers, prior scores and observed claims cannot bias the listening task, and actual serialized context is retained

#### Scenario: I33 Ambiguous expectations and valid alternatives
- **WHEN** a fixture cannot establish that a full form or contextual contour is incorrect
- **THEN** its withheld expectation can remain uncertain even if a model supports the intended error; correct strong forms, accents, careful linking and legitimate question contours stay acceptable, false alarms prevent confirmation and a failed observation cannot become a successful contrast

#### Scenario: I33 Saved physical and contextual evidence
- **WHEN** QUAL-04's five saved sets are inspected
- **THEN** every original target and three controls retains its exact context/audio/reply; the actual 14400-sample silence and retained opposed F0 windows remain distinct from functional judgment, the missing car in the future intonation context is repaired, and changed inputs are not labelled as newly observed

#### Scenario: I33 Zero-call reconciliation and preservation
- **WHEN** iteration33 research is finalized
- **THEN** directly affected literal tests, saved native inputs/outputs and local Lab export/readback are reconciled without new inference or synthesis; mixed observations remain visible and neither diagnostics nor fixture repair changes learner keys/scores, original categories/formulas/source scopes or user data
