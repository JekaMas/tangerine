## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: COV-001 grammar.determiners.article_omission

The full release SHALL cover `grammar.determiners.article_omission` as a source-defined error opportunity in `grammar` using `textual`. Its exact inclusion/exclusion, context, correction, render, verification and scoring contracts in specification/v2/domain/error-taxonomy.v1.yaml SHALL be preserved. Development/calibration/holdout coverage SHALL be tracked separately under the approved counting in specification/v2/docs/review-and-gold.md; the inherited six-per-leaf/732 policy SHALL not be reduced or treated as achieved. LLM-labelled examples and imported native labels SHALL retain their origin; full coverage is a report, not a normal-use gate.

#### Scenario: COV-001 Complete supported behavior

- **GIVEN** Use actual audio and machine/native labels with explicit origin for grammar.determiners.article_omission, its source-defined exclusions and required control/contrast; freeze final hash and disjoint groups.
- **WHEN** the authorized owner checks the full frozen contract
- **THEN** Coverage for grammar.determiners.article_omission names actual eligible observations, controls, assets and split case IDs under the approved counting rule.

#### Scenario: COV-001 Incomplete or forbidden behavior

- **GIVEN** an omitted, altered or unauthorized dependency in that same contract
- **WHEN** the caller requests acceptance
- **THEN** Wrong-context or excluded grammar.determiners.article_omission labels, unlabelled examples, absent acoustic realization, duplicate labels/assets or invented observations cannot fill its denominator.
