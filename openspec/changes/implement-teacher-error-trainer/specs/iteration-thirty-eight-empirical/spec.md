## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: I38 Produce source-specific empirical reports from imported data
Tangerine SHALL use its existing empirical calculation and immutable-report storage owners to produce coverage, native-label frequency and relationship reports from available imported populations. Source/release/view/split/annotator and denominator meanings SHALL remain explicit. Conservative versioned derived category mappings SHALL preserve unmapped and ambiguous events. Unsupported rates SHALL remain unknown. Overlapping views, alternatives, automatic alignments, ASR and ratings SHALL not become independent populations or invented mistake labels. No model call, empirical all-category promise or corpus prerequisite SHALL be added to ordinary practice. The ordinary report flow SHALL use named choices and readable language without editing identifiers, mappings or JSON.

#### Scenario: I38 Select saved source populations
- **WHEN** an operator opens report choices after source import
- **THEN** published completed imports and their supported named views are selectable with actual provenance and explicit constructed-example labels; source versions and overlapping views remain separate and empty/unavailable choices do not block saved reports or practice

#### Scenario: I38 Native writing populations and denominators
- **WHEN** a native FCE or W&I M2 view is selected
- **THEN** its exact accepted sentences, tokens and one selected native annotator supply recorded edits and no-error observations; invalid, unresolved, absent-annotator and withheld-label observations retain honest exclusions and unknowns

#### Scenario: I38 Conservative localized category mappings
- **WHEN** native labels and source spans support a documented category mapping
- **THEN** the saved versioned crosswalk records native and derived meanings separately, ambiguous mappings stay unassigned and the report preserves all 122 categories without claiming uninspected absence

#### Scenario: I38 Manual speech annotations
- **WHEN** the L2-ARCTIC manual phone population is selected
- **THEN** utterance windows, source phone operations and unchanged observations remain separate; automatic alignment, comments, silence and uncertain phone meaning do not become invented pronunciation mistakes or verified audio facts

#### Scenario: I38 Coverage without localized error labels
- **WHEN** JFLEG, SpeechOcean or a speech/dialogue supplement lacks the selected localized error annotations
- **THEN** actual references, ratings or contextual records supply descriptive coverage, while error frequencies/relationships remain unavailable and alternatives, accents, ASR, repeated views and release versions are not counted as independent errors

#### Scenario: I38 Exact native and mapped relationships
- **WHEN** inspected source windows support native-label frequencies and distinct-event co-occurrence
- **THEN** the existing exact arithmetic supplies separate native and mapped results with explicit denominators, exclusions and scope; empty/zero/unknown inputs do not produce invented rates, absence or causal relationships

#### Scenario: I38 Durable reports and recovery
- **WHEN** a source report is calculated, reopened or its transport fails
- **THEN** the immutable input/output and source/mapping lineage persist, readback and reuse of saved input do not reselect current source rows, reapply a new mapping or re-audit admitted data, transient reads log/retry with retained state and final guidance is authored; no model or paid call occurs

#### Scenario: I38 Readable ordinary report workflow
- **WHEN** an operator selects a named population, creates, reads and downloads a report
- **THEN** coverage, counts, denominators, mappings, unknowns and relationships are understandable without technical payloads; keyboard/reflow/recovery and real screenshot/video evidence cover populated, empty, loading, success and error states
