## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: CV-01 Complete category evidence matrix
The application development tooling SHALL enumerate every original category and SHALL distinguish native implementation/grading evidence from actual final-audio confirmation for each explicit backend/model/voice/accent binding. Unselected and unavailable audio observations SHALL remain visible without an invented pass.

#### Scenario: CV-01 Covered implementation and missing recording
- **WHEN** a category has valid native evidence but no selected actual recording observation
- **THEN** the matrix retains that implementation evidence and reports audio as not_run
- **AND** another category or voice cannot supply its missing confirmation

### Requirement: CV-02 Blind actual-audio probes
The existing auditory owner SHALL support a separate development probe containing actual randomized numbered clips, public context and questions. Its model-visible projection SHALL exclude private roles, key, expected verdicts, intended IPA and misleading mutation claims while preserving complete native context/output evidence.

#### Scenario: CV-02 Clean and falsely labelled clean audio
- **WHEN** clean, mutated, acceptable and falsely labelled clean recordings enter a probe
- **THEN** the model receives neutral clip IDs and actual audio in matching order
- **AND** changing only private mutation metadata cannot alter the model-visible request

### Requirement: CV-03 Honest realization outcomes and false alarms
The comparison owner SHALL require a correct mutation judgment and passing correct controls before reporting confirmed. Missing, conflicting, unsupported and uncertain model findings SHALL not establish realization. A false alarm on a correct accent or clean clip SHALL prevent confirmation for that tested binding.

#### Scenario: CV-03 Requested edit without an audible contrast
- **WHEN** the model contradicts the intended mutation or falsely accuses a correct control
- **THEN** the result retains that actual finding as not_realized or uncertain
- **AND** a successful renderer, manifest or receipt cannot convert it to confirmed

### Requirement: CV-04 One check with persisted reuse
An explicit validation action SHALL persist its native input/output and comparison. Later reads and resumes of that unchanged experiment SHALL reuse the saved observation without model calls or repeated stored-byte checks. A changed binding SHALL use an explicit separate experiment.

#### Scenario: CV-04 Saved observation and unavailable provider
- **WHEN** a completed experiment is read while its provider is unavailable
- **THEN** its saved result remains readable without dispatch
- **AND** an unperformed observation remains not_run rather than a fabricated cached pass

### Requirement: CV-05 Evidence appropriate to the acoustic claim
Numeric timing, pitch and rhythm confirmation SHALL require actual scoped waveform observations as well as listening evidence. Symbolic intended values, transcription and punctuation SHALL not substitute for measured sound. Correct accent, strong/weak, careful/blended and contextual intonation alternatives SHALL remain controls.

#### Scenario: CV-05 Correct auditory label with missing physical evidence
- **WHEN** a numeric acoustic target has a supported model reply but no actual physical observation
- **THEN** confirmation remains uncertain
- **AND** a supplied actual matching physical observation may complete that finite contrast without claiming universal perception
- **AND** a supporting physical observation and contradictory auditory judgment remain an explicit uncertainty with both observations preserved

### Requirement: CV-06 Actual finite execution and retained proof limits
Selected actual local/free/included execution SHALL retain the final WAVs, independent expected outcomes, model bindings, prompt/context, failures and local Lab artifacts. Every category SHALL have executable positive and negative comparison cases. Implementation tests SHALL not be described as exhaustive synthesized or perceptually qualified audio.

#### Scenario: CV-06 Finite runtime and complete inventory
- **WHEN** the executable finite population and selected real recordings are evaluated
- **THEN** exact passing/failing/uncertain/unperformed identities remain available in the existing DAG and category matrix
- **AND** a selected successful subset cannot be presented as every subtype, voice, accent or provider passing
