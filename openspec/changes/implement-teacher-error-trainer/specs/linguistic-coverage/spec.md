## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: LF-01 Complete finite taxonomy and contextual exclusions
The application SHALL preserve every original taxonomy ID and SHALL provide explicit subtype scope and concrete correct controls for every leaf. Existing selection and interpretation context SHALL carry the selected definitions and exclusions. A category incompatible with explicitly selected families SHALL remain rejected before inference. The finite inventory SHALL not claim to enumerate all possible English or all accents.

#### Scenario: LF-01 Selected leaf and incompatible family
- **WHEN** a registered leaf is selected for an ordinary preparation
- **THEN** its subtype scope and literal correct control reach the existing generation and mapper context
- **AND** a conflicting explicit family rejects that selection without inventing another category

### Requirement: LF-02 Correct variants remain acceptable
The application SHALL distinguish a contextual defect, an explicit practice-target mismatch and a permitted variety/register/sense. Accents, allophones, connected or careful speech, licensed grammar and lexical/collocation alternatives SHALL not create errors solely by differing from a preferred form. Confirming a control SHALL create no true-error opportunity; calling it wrong SHALL be a false positive under the existing arithmetic.

#### Scenario: LF-02 Correct control versus unnecessary correction
- **WHEN** the listener correctly accepts a contextual control
- **THEN** it creates no true error, miss or false positive
- **AND** an unsupported assertion that the same control is wrong counts one false positive and no detected error

### Requirement: LF-03 Independent components and concrete mutation examples
The application SHALL preserve exact correction/detection credit independently of optional explanations. A supplied false universal rule SHALL receive its own negative explanation judgment; an omitted explanation SHALL have no penalty. Active mutation illustrations SHALL have concrete contextual contrasts and replayable written edits; ambiguous historical illustrations SHALL remain preserved as historical inputs rather than unquestioned truth.

#### Scenario: LF-03 Valid primary answer and false or omitted reason
- **WHEN** a supported error is detected and correctly repaired
- **THEN** absent optional reasoning does not cancel that credit
- **AND** a supplied false reason receives zero explanation credit without cancelling the independently supported primary components
- **AND** the declared written edit produces its literal surface example inside its declared surface scope

### Requirement: LF-04 Falsifiable linguistic development evidence
Development evidence SHALL enumerate the selected finite population, independent expected answers and actual native/model results. The candidate SHALL not receive expected labels, taxonomy-derived status or reference correction/control reasons. Symbolic IPA/timing and model opinions SHALL not be described as independently heard generated audio. Missing acoustic evidence SHALL remain unknown. Research, failed attacks, repairs, retained observations and proof limits SHALL be recorded in the tracked audit and the existing DAG; no new runtime gate or human review is added.

#### Scenario: LF-04 Correct, incorrect and unavailable-evidence propositions
- **WHEN** the finite linguistic contrast population is tested through the native included/local/free model and diagnostic owners
- **THEN** correct variants and false universal rules are compared against independently authored labels withheld from model input
- **AND** an assertion about actual sound with no audio/measurement remains unknown with its missing evidence stated
- **AND** a successful request or requested operation cannot substitute for the claimed linguistic or acoustic result

### Requirement: LF-05 Missing subtypes reuse the actual injection owner
The existing phone-pair compiler SHALL support declared consonant-order/metathesis and internal vowel-elision contrasts in their selected scope, preserving the original surface wording and one independently repairable unit. The reverse permitted variant SHALL remain representable. Unsupported model phones SHALL fail honestly rather than be silently substituted. Compiled phones SHALL not be described as heard output.

#### Scenario: LF-05 Ordered phone contrast and permitted variant
- **WHEN** an explicitly scoped consonant-order or internal-vowel contrast is compiled
- **THEN** its full reference and replacement sequences preserve the intended order and unchanged text
- **AND** the reverse allowed variant remains renderable without creating an error judgment
- **AND** a phone absent from the selected vocabulary is rejected instead of silently changed

### Requirement: LF-06 Changed prompts have bounded actual flow evidence
The affected shipped generation, mapping, optional mapping audit, case-review and adjudication prompts SHALL have actual run-scoped native/context/output evidence for the selected development flow. A valid detected correction and a false accusation about an acceptable form SHALL produce the existing exact counts, separate explanation feedback and grounded recommendations. Stage-only optional review observations SHALL not be described as a published review/result revision. Previously saved user exercises SHALL remain untouched.

#### Scenario: LF-06 On-demand exercise with correct and overcorrected answers
- **WHEN** the selected native included/local setup generates and mutates a new exercise, stores speech and grades a response containing a valid repair and an unnecessary correction of a permitted form
- **THEN** actual text/audio/key/answer/model/context artifacts and exact supported scores are retained
- **AND** false universal explanations receive separate feedback without cancelling the valid primary repair
- **AND** selected optional review/reconsideration stages retain actual judgments with their precise stage-only proof boundary
